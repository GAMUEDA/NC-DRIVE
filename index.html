    Spacing

    Parts only sit at 0° or 180° (or the whole nest at 90°/270°) to respect grain direction, never mixed. Parts are never mirrored, so etching stays on the right side.
    Parts fill from the sheet's zero, the bottom-right corner: bottom row first, right to left. Tick “Turn 90°” on a part to turn just that part.
    Full-length nests keep a clear lane at each 4' mark (a 10' sheet = 3 sections, a 14' sheet = 4) with a line drawn there, so the sheet can be cut into handleable pieces. If a part is longer than 4', that job isn't sectioned. The lines are not in the .nc.
    One straight cut across the usable width above the tallest part frees the unused strip in one piece. On the 4' × 10', a gauge with a part longer than 10' moves to a 4' × 14' sheet by itself.

    NC output
