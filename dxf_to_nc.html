Turret parts (14443 …): each DXF makes four AMADA programs, - 1 one part, - 2 two, - 3 three and - 4 a full 48" sheet, each with its .nc and its .pdf program sheet. Other parts are converted with nest_admin 5's program and its default conditions: stray geometry, bend lines and feature outlines removed, etched text found and marked, and parts on the never-programmed (OMIT) list refused. Type a part number or drop the DXF files, then press RUN CONVERSION. Each DXF becomes its own .nc with the same name, zeroed at the part's lower-left corner (G92), metric, incremental, holes before the outer profile, etching as M11/M09. Feed and power are set on the machine. ⬇ on a row downloads that one file.
